# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

This policy covers **You Hypothetically Uploader** ("the App"), a private desktop tool used by the owner of the YouTube channels You, Hypothetically and Your Call to upload and schedule the channels' own videos through the YouTube Data API and to see how those videos are doing.

## 1. Who uses the App
The App is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

## 2. What Google data the App accesses
With the owner's consent, the App requests these Google OAuth scopes:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload` — to upload videos to the owner's channels.
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl` — to set a video's details and scheduled publication time, and to read the status and statistics of the owner's own videos.

The App reads only the minimum it needs: the channel's name (to confirm the correct channel was authorised), the status of videos it uploaded, and the statistics of the channels and their recent videos, such as subscriber and view counts.

## 3. How the data is used
The data is used solely to upload the channels' videos, set their titles, descriptions and publication times, confirm that uploads succeeded, and show the owner the channels' statistics on the owner's own computer. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## 4. Storage and security
The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any server other than Google's. Upload records and the statistics history are also kept only on the owner's computer. The App has no server, database or cloud storage of its own.

## 5. Sharing
The App does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any Google user data to any third party.

## 6. Retention and deletion
Tokens and local records remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions); deleting the local files removes all stored data.

## 7. YouTube and Google
By using the App, the owner is bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
